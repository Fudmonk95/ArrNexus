Spotify setup checklist
  1. Create/open your Spotify developer application and copy its Client ID + Client Secret.
  2. Add {{ spotify_redirect_uri }} as a Redirect URI in Spotify. Matching is exact.
  3. For Spotify Development Mode, add the Spotify account under Users Management/allowlist and check Spotify's current Premium/quota requirements.
  4. Save this page, then go to Music Hub → Spotify → Connect Spotify for each ArrNexus user.
