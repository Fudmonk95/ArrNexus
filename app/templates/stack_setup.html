How should ArrNexus manage this server?

For this server, adoption is the safe choice: keep the working configs and progressively bring containers under ArrNexus control.
No big-bang migration. Running Docker containers cannot be moved into a different Compose project without recreation. Adoption records the existing live state first; each service can then cross the ArrNexus ownership boundary one at a time with rollback.
